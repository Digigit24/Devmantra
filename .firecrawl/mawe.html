    From The Chairperson Desk

    Ms. Archana Bhatnagar
    Chairperson MAWE
    Managing Director Haylide Chemicals Pvt Ltd
    President (MP State Council)WICCI

    A little above the bottom of the pyramid lies a huge section of educated but unemployed women. These women, if guided can become a great source for increasing employment for others, usually other women, and could play a major role in the country’s economy.       A Journey of 24 years now in MAWE of having led thousands of women to be entrepreneurs or hand hold existing entrepreneurship to help them transit to the next level. To inspire them at each steps, I guess has come from my journey of success & failure of 43 years.
