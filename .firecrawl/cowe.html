Confederation of Women Entrepreneurs (COWE India)

COWE India is a premier not-for-profit organization established on March 11, 2005, by six successful first-generation women entrepreneurs with a vision to drive economic empowerment for women across India. What started with 22 committed members in Hyderabad has now transformed into a national movement.

Our vision

To be a national association of women business owners that propels women entrepreneurs into economic and political spheres of power.

COWE: Elevating Women in Business through Catalyzing Entrepreneurship

At COWE, we understand the unique challenges women face in business, hence we're committed towards supporting women entrepreneurs, from startup to scale-up. We're here to nurture your venture's growth, help overcome obstacles, and unlock opportunities.
We invite women entrepreneurs to join our community and leverage our resources, network, and expertise to achieve their dreams. Together, let's create a thriving ecosystem that empowers women to succeed.
We also welcome like-minded organizations and individuals to collaborate with us, support our mission, and amplify the impact. Let's work together to elevate Women in Business. - Nidhi Toshniwal National President, COWE India
