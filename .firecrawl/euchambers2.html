  • Unique Representation: The only Chamber in India covering all EU member states.
  • Pan-India and Europe Reach: Over 1,100 members from key sectors and regions.
  • Institutional Linkages: Access to Indian Ministries, EU Embassies, Trade Promotion Councils, and Government Agencies.
  • Business-Centric Programs: Tailored events, summits, and curated B2B engagement platforms.
  • Increased Visibility: Promotion through leading publications, social media and newsletters.
