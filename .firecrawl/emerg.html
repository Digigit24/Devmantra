Our approach

Congratulations on discovering eMERG, a non-profit organization that empowers women entrepreneurs and professionals through training and sharing industry knowledge. You are embarking on an exciting journey, alongside like-minded individuals, who are dedicated to revolutionizing technology, business, and services. eMERG, together with government and non-government departments, is here to support you and help you reach your full potential. With eMERG’s resources and guidance, you can take a significant step towards becoming a leader in your field. Let us come together to shape a world where women can achieve greatness and pave the way for others to follow. Welcome to eMERG – where every woman is equipped to thrive.

 

eMERG is...

At eMERG, we are committed to building an inclusive ecosystem that empowers women entrepreneurs and professionals. As we evolve, we are introducing new initiatives and reimagining our events and services to better serve our members. Our focus remains on enabling growth through collaboration, knowledge sharing, and market linkages, while fostering innovation and creating meaningful opportunities for women-led enterprises.

Ms Radhika Setty, President eMERG

eMERG FOR YOU

You need the right support behind you and your dream!

Business Networking

With more than 300+ entrepreneurs with eMERG networking is super easy. You might find next big customer to be part of this group. At eMERG we also provide networking opportunities

Coaching & Mentoring

At eMERG we offer coaching and mentoring, skill development sessions to our members. With the entrepreneurial experience our senior member help newer members by mentoring and coaching them in business

Events & Seminars

eMERG has its flagship event called FLLN (Forging Links and Leveraging Networks) and many other events, seminars conducted for members. Apart from that, for a member we also open up events and seminars both International and domestic in collaboration with our partners

Trade Delegations, Government Agency Support

eMERG has its network with government agencies, business groups, women consortiums and many other external agencies and partners. We would help our members to be part of the trade delegations, investor meets, summits for business promotions
