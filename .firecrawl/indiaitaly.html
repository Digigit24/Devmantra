    OUR OFFICES

    ABOUT US

    Established in Mumbai in 1966, the IICCI - Indo-Italian Chamber of Commerce and Industry, IndiaItaly is an association of  750 + companies, professionals, individuals and intermediate bodies.

    ​

    We are officially recognized by the Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy and we are members of Assocamerestero, the Association of the Italian Chambers in the World.

     

    Today, we count on a network of 7 offices in India (Mumbai - head office, Delhi, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Chennai, Pune and Ahmedabad) and 2 Antennas in Italy.
